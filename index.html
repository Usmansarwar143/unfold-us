Services

What we do

Every engagement follows the same order. First we unfold how the business actually runs, then we build around that reality, then the product grows in step with the business.

01
Unfold

We map how the business actually runs. Workflows, handoffs and pain points are opened up and documented, so the real process is visible to everyone before anything is designed.

This stage replaces assumptions with a shared picture of the work, including the manual steps and workarounds your team relies on today.

02
Build

We design and build the SaaS product around that reality. The software follows the workflow your team already knows, shaped to remove the manual steps that slow the work down.

Design and development stay tied to the mapped process at every stage, so the finished product fits the business that will use it daily.

03
Scale

The product grows in step with the business. As the team, the volume of work and the needs of the operation grow, the software is extended and refined to match.

Scaling is planned from the start, so growth adds capability to the same clear tool your team already uses.

Who it is for

Built for teams that have outgrown spreadsheets

UnfoldUS works with founders and operations leads at growing teams of 5 to 200 people who have outgrown spreadsheets and off the shelf tools. The work usually starts when a process that once lived in one file or one inbox now passes through several people, and nobody has a single clear view of it.

If your team spends time copying information between tools, chasing updates by message, or rebuilding the same report every week, that workflow is the starting point for a conversation with us.

Process

How we work

A clear sequence, followed on every project. You always know which stage the work is in and what comes next.

Step 1

Conversation and mapping

We start by talking with you and the people who do the work, then map how work currently moves through the business, step by step. The map covers workflows, handoffs and pain points, and it becomes the reference for everything that follows.

Step 2

Design around real workflows

We shape the product around the mapped workflows. Screens, actions and reports reflect how your team actually works, reviewed with you before building begins in earnest.

Step 3

Build in stages

We build the product in stages, sharing progress as each part takes shape. You respond to working software rather than documents, and each stage is checked against the original map of your workflow.

Step 4

Launch and scale with the business

We launch the product with your team and support its use from the first day. As the business grows and its needs change, we extend and refine the product so it keeps fitting the way you work.
