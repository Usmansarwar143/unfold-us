# UnfoldUS Logo Set, Emerald Edition

One folder, one file per logo variant. Colours: Midnight Navy #0A1931, Emerald Green #0B8A5F, Pearl #F7F5F0. Solid colours only.

1. unfoldus-logo-01-primary-u2s-light.webp
   Primary logo. U2S monogram plus full wordmark and tagline, navy and emerald on pearl. Main logo for website header, documents and presentations.

2. unfoldus-logo-02-appicon-usquare.webp
   App icon. Navy square tile, white U inside corner brackets, emerald folded corner, emerald S. For app icons, favicons and social avatars.

3. unfoldus-logo-03-dark-horizontal.webp
   Dark horizontal lockup. White monogram and wordmark with emerald accents on Midnight Navy, single line. For dark footers, video and dark sections.

4. unfoldus-logo-04-wordmark-only.webp
   Wordmark only. UnfoldUS wordmark plus tagline, no symbol. For tight spaces, partner rows and document footers. Note: this presentation file rendered on a warm beige tint; redraw on Pearl #F7F5F0 for strict brand use.

Tagline: Unfold. Build. Scale.
The superscript 2 stands for the two U letters in UnfoldUS, U squared plus S.
